# Goal 010 — Productize ping Level 4 socket reconstruction

## Status

Completed in this change. Superseded by Goal 011 as the primary product surface: ping Level 4 support now goes through the portable machine `machinen snapshot` / `machinen restore` path.

## Purpose

Graduate the accepted Goal 003 ping slice from proof evidence to a narrow product-supported Level 4 descriptor route.

This is intentionally smaller than "all ping sockets". The product-supported subset is only:

- target-native recreation of a datagram ping socket or raw ICMP socket;
- loopback route and target loopback namespace;
- preserved ICMP echo identifier and next sequence;
- empty receive queue;
- no in-flight ICMP packets;
- no active `recvmsg` frame;
- a target credential/capability mapping (`ping_group_range` or `CAP_NET_RAW`);
- target-native verifier evidence at restore time.

## Public surfaces

- `machinen capture ping-socket --out <dir> --source-arch <arm64|amd64> --target-arch <arm64|amd64> --socket-kind <ping-dgram-icmp|raw-icmp> --source-verifier-output <file> --echo-id <n> --echo-seq <n>`
- `MACHINEN_SNAPSHOT_ENGINE=portable machinen snapshot <vm> <bundle>` (Goal 011 primary product surface)
- `machinen restore <portable-ping-socket-bundle> --target-arch <arm64|amd64> [--target-verifier-output <file>]`
- `machinen support --profile ping-level4-socket-reconstruction-v1 --json`

## Stable refusals preserved

The following neighbors remain unsupported with `migrationCompleted=false`:

- unread receive queue;
- in-flight ICMP packets;
- active `recvmsg`;
- ambiguous route or network namespace;
- missing credential/capability mapping;
- unsupported raw socket options;
- target verifier mismatch.
